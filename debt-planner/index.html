Your plan

Avalanche

Highest interest rate first. You tackle the most expensive debt before cheaper ones. This always costs the least interest overall, or ties.

Snowball

Smallest balance first. You clear small debts quickly, whatever their rate. It usually costs a little more, but the early wins help many people stay on track.

    Questions about paying off debt

    Which debt should I pay off first?

    Priority debts first: rent or mortgage, council tax, energy, court fines, child maintenance and HMRC. Missing these can lead to eviction, enforcement agents or losing your supply. After that, paying the highest interest rate first (avalanche) costs the least.

    Avalanche or snowball?

    Avalanche targets the highest APR and almost always saves money. Snowball targets the smallest balance, so you clear debts sooner and see progress faster. If the difference in cost is small, pick the one you'll stick with.

    What if my budget doesn't cover the minimums?

    Speak to a free debt adviser before you miss payments. They can talk to lenders for you and explain options such as breathing space, a debt management plan or a debt relief order. You should never need to pay for debt advice.

    How accurate is this?

    It assumes rates and minimum payments stay the same and that you add no new borrowing. Real minimums often fall as balances fall, and 0% deals end, so treat the dates as a guide and rerun the plan when things change.
